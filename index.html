FOUNDER & MANAGING DIRECTOR

Meet the Leading Force Behind BNP Interiors

Mr. Punam D. Kularia — Founder & Managing Director

Founded in 2006 under the visionary leadership of Mr. Punam D. Kularia, BNP Interiors has grown into one of India’s premier turnkey civil and interior contracting powerhouses. Built on an unwavering foundation of transparency, engineering excellence, and execution integrity, he has directed over 700+ landmark projects across hospitality, corporate, commercial, and high-end residential sectors nationwide.

With a 5,000+ strong workforce and a state-of-the-art Navi Mumbai manufacturing facility equipped with German (HOMAG) and Italian automated machinery, Mr. Kularia continues to steer the firm with a philosophy centered on precision, lasting relationships, and on-time project delivery.

MILESTONES OF EXCELLENCE

Our Journey Through The Years

From our founding in 2006 to delivering 700+ turnkey civil and interior landmarks across India, explore the defining milestones of BNP Interiors.

2006 ₹9 CR CONTRACT

Inception & First Landmark

Company founded by Mr. Punamchand Kularia on November 8, 2006. Launched with a ₹9 Crore landmark project from Shoppers Stop, completed with 600 skilled craftsmen from Rajasthan in record time.

Shoppers Stop Retail Turnkey
2008 13 LAKH SQ.FT

Mumbai HQ & Asia's Largest Mall Project

Established Mumbai Headquarters. Signed India's largest single mall project — 1.3 Million Sq. Ft. with DB Mall. Entered the Banking sector with Citibank & BNP Paribas (pan-India relationship continuing to date).

DB Mall BNP Paribas Citibank
2009 BANKING SECTOR

Forayed into Banking Sector

Deepened engagement with Citibank & BNP Paribas pan-India, executing branch rollouts and corporate office fit-outs across major metro cities. Established BNP Interiors as a trusted BFSI interior contractor.

Citibank BNP Paribas BFSI Expansion
2010 MULTI-SECTOR

Commercial & Retail Expansion

Scaled operations significantly with a growing portfolio of retail malls, commercial offices, and banking branches. Built a skilled workforce of over 1,000 artisans and project professionals across India.

Retail Malls Commercial Offices 1,000+ Workforce
2011 MADE IN INDIA

First State-of-the-Art Furniture Factory, Mumbai

Made a single largest machinery order from Germany & Italy. Became the first turnkey interior company in Mumbai to set up an advanced in-house furniture manufacturing facility using HOMAG technology.

HOMAG Germany Italian Machinery Factory Setup
2012 HOSPITALITY ENTRY

Hospitality Sector Breakthrough

With the factory now fully operational, BNP Interiors expanded aggressively into 5-star hospitality. Executed landmark hotel interiors across major cities, cementing BNP's reputation for luxury grade finishes.

5-Star Hospitality In-House Furniture
2013 300 KEYS WIN

Novotel Pune & Massive Commercial Mandates

Massive breakthrough in hospitality with a 300 keys Novotel project. Simultaneously executed UST Global's 9 Lakh Sq.Ft. office space. BNP's quality convinced Accor to cancel China imports and award BNP the full contract.

Novotel Pune UST Global Kerala Accor Hotels
2014 MAKE IN INDIA

National Conclave Honor — Vigyan Bhawan

Vigyan Bhawan Segment Leader Make In India
2016 MARRIOTT & HYATT

Two Consecutive Hotel Wins — Marriott & Hyatt

Won two consecutive hotel project mandates under Marriott International & Hyatt International — a defining moment that positioned BNP Interiors among India's top 5-star hospitality interior contractors.

Marriott International Hyatt International
2018 5-STAR TAJ WINS

Vivanta Bhubaneswar & Cidade de Goa

Successfully completed Vivanta by Taj Bhubaneswar and the iconic Cidade de Goa. Alongside delivered major commercial offices, malls, and retail spaces while continuing pan-India BFSI mandates with HDFC, ICICI & HSBC.

Vivanta by Taj Cidade de Goa HDFC · ICICI · HSBC
2019 ₹100 CR ORDER

Hyatt Regency Mega Win & HICSA Award

Secured a mega ₹100 Crore single order into the hospitality sector — Hyatt Regency. Recognised and awarded at HICSA 2019 (Hotel of the Year) in Chennai, Tamil Nadu — one of India's most prestigious hospitality industry awards.

Hyatt Regency HICSA 2019 Winner ₹100 Cr Order
2020 2,500 ROOMS

2,500 Rooms & Pandemic Resilience

Completed 2,500 guestrooms under Samhi Hotels. Maintained a workforce of 1,200 people active under strict lockdown to deliver HDFC & BNP Paribas mandates on time — a testament to BNP's unmatched delivery commitment.

Samhi Hotels 1,200 People Active Pandemic Resilience
2021 4,000+ GUESTROOMS

Industry Leader — 4,000+ Guestrooms Completed

Established as a true industry leader by completing over 4,000 total guestrooms — a milestone no Mumbai-based interior contractor had achieved. Recognised with the Uddyami Samman Award for entrepreneurial excellence.

4,000+ Guestrooms Uddyami Samman Award
2022 TAJ AMER WIN

Taj Amer Jaipur & Taj Mahal Palace Mumbai

Swift execution of Taj Amer Jaipur (275 keys, 700 artisans deployed) and the iconic Taj Mahal Palace Mumbai. Received mandate for the regal 5-star Taj Gorbandh Palace Jaisalmer — another crown jewel win under IHCL.

Taj Amer Jaipur Taj Mahal Palace Taj Gorbandh Jaisalmer
2023 5 LAC SQ.FT HDFC

Taj Puri, DAIS BKC & Indian Accent

Received mandate for Taj Jaisalmer & Taj Puri Resort. Won 5 Lakh Sq.Ft. HDFC Kolkata HQ. Contracted for high-end members clubhouse in Hyderabad. Completed Dhirubhai Ambani International School (DAIS) BKC and handed over Indian Accent — recognised as the best Indian restaurant globally.

Taj Puri Resort DAIS BKC Indian Accent HDFC Kolkata
2024 1M+ SQ.FT BFSI

Radisson Jamshedpur, PMO & BFSI Surge

Awarded 120 guest rooms for Radisson Hotel Jamshedpur. Delivered 1 Million+ Sq.Ft. of BFSI works in a single year. Worked alongside L&T for government projects including Seva Teerth (Prime Minister's Office). Completed high-end residential projects with HDFC.

Radisson Jamshedpur PMO Seva Teerth L&T Collaboration
2025 ULTRA-LUXURY

Taj Chambers, Taj Ganga Kutir & 27-Acre Factory

Secured mandates for ultra-luxury hospitality projects — Taj Chambers & Taj Ganga Kutir Resort & Spa — crafting bespoke furniture in-house with German-engineered machinery. Chosen to shape regal experiences of Taj properties. 27-acre BNP Factory Craft Village development commences in Rajasthan. Featured in SOH Magazine's Brand Edit.

Taj Chambers Taj Ganga Kutir 27-Acre Craft Village
2026 PM INAUGURATION

Seva Teerth PMO Inaugurated by PM Modi

A historic landmark — on 13th February 2026, Hon'ble Prime Minister Narendra Modi Ji inaugurated Seva Teerth (Prime Minister's Office), a prestigious project delivered with pride and honour by BNP Interiors. As we step into 2026, we look forward to delivering even more prestigious projects.
