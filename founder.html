FOUNDER JOURNEY

Punamchand D. Kularia

Founder & Managing Director, BNP Interiors

SOH COVER SPECIAL Soul of Hospitality
ROOTED IN TRADITION

Ancestral Craftsmanship,
Driven by Passion

"Sansar mein vahi shresht hain jo apne kartavya ka imandari purvak palan karte huye karm path par hamesha agrasar rehte hain..." — Santh Shri Dularam Ji Kularia (Spiritual Guide & Father)

Punam Kularia hails from a traditional Marwari family of Rajasthan, where business acumen and structural woodwork are second nature. His father, Sant Dularam Kularia, was a respected suthar (carpenter), and it was through watching him work with dedication and precision that Punam absorbed the values of trust, craftsmanship, and quality.

After studying commerce, Punam joined the family's interior business in 2001 at the age of 24, driven by a vision to create something distinct. "I learned by observing my father and brothers. Those early years were all about absorbing."

By 2006, he launched BNP Interiors, which has since transformed over 650+ spaces across India, establishing a benchmark for luxury contracting and industrial manufacturing.

PRINCIPLES OF LEADERSHIP

My Success Mantras

These five core principles govern our turnkey contracting practice and client relationships at BNP Interiors:

1

Always Be Available: Prioritize client communication and site updates. Passion fuels work — without it, do not start.

2

Owner First: Never say no to a client's design adjustments. Accommodate alterations transparently without budget overruns.

3

Follow Love & Passion: Hire craftsmen and professionals who share the absolute love for architectural execution.

4

Leverage Strengths: Match the right craftsmen to the right details. Detail-oriented joiners vs. fast general executors.

5

Honest Commitments: Build long-term relationships based on structural transparency. Repeat business is our biggest reward.

"My day begins at 7:00 a.m. — checking site updates with every project lead. I stay on-site until the job is done. Passion and clarity keep fatigue at bay."
