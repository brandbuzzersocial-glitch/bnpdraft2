FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions About Our Process

Q: What's a typical project timeline?

Timelines vary by scope — a five-star hotel fit-out can take several months to over a year, while a corporate office may take 8–16 weeks. We've delivered 150,000 sq. ft. hospitality projects in as little as five months and produced a full hotel mock-up room in 21 days, so we scope realistic timelines during the consultation stage.

Q: What does your process look like?

We follow a four-stage turnkey process: Consultation & Feasibility → Design & Visualization → Procurement & Production → Execution & Handover — all tracked through our in-house ERP system covering tendering, planning, procurement, and accounts.

Q: How are budgets structured?

Because we manage both civil execution and in-house furniture manufacturing, we can offer a single turnkey quotation covering design, materials, fabrication and installation — reducing coordination costs versus managing multiple vendors separately.

Q: Can you work with existing furniture or partial fit-outs?

Yes. We regularly integrate new interiors with existing structures and furnishings, particularly for renovation and phased hospitality projects — this is scoped during the initial site assessment.
