ABOUT OUR STUDIO

25+ Years of Mastering Luxury With Vision and Precision

Founded in 2006 by Mr. Punamchand Kularia, BNP Interiors began its journey with a ₹9 crore landmark win from Shoppers Stop. What started as a modest venture has grown into one of India's leading civil and interior general contracting firms — delivering over 700 projects across hospitality, banking, corporate, mall/retail, restaurant, residential, healthcare, and education sectors.

Our Vision

To be one of the most trusted, distinguished, honest and ethical Civil & Interior General Contracting companies — as we move toward our goal of being the best Interior Fit-Out and Furniture Manufacturing company in the country.

To deliver high-quality, cost-effective projects on schedule, implementing the best business practices, using state-of-the-art machinery and efficient, flexible, focused teams across civil and interior disciplines — bringing efficiency to scale with cost-effective solutions.

OUR METHODOLOGY

A Seamless Path From Concept To Completion

Our 4-stage turnkey process ensures complete transparency and adherence to timelines through our in-house ERP system.

01

Consultation & Site Assessment

We analyze brief requirements, site conditions, technical specifications, and budgetary boundaries.

02

Concept, Design & Visualization

Developing 2D drawings, 3D photorealistic visual models, BIM coordination, and material selection.

03

Procurement & Production

In-house precision manufacturing run in parallel with site civil & MEP works tracked through ERP.

04

Execution & Handover

On-site installation, stringent quality control audits, final walkthrough, and post-handover support.
